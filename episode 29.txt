We had a yummy homemade peach crisp for episode 29 this week!! Nothing says summer morning than coffee and a sweet fruit dish ☀️🍑☕️

Recipe:
About 10 peaches
Cinnamon
Nutmeg
Lemon Juice
Cornstarch
Rolled oats(M toasted hers in a skillet first with more warm spices!)
Cold cubed butter
Preheat your oven to 350F and bake for 15-18 minutes until golden and bubbling!
