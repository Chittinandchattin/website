We touch on the topic of the moon and her phases in episode 23! Come and journey into these phases💜

the moon isn’t just pretty — it’s actually doing something to you 🌕✨

the moon controls the tides, and since our bodies are made of roughly 60% water, it makes sense that it affects us too. science has actually caught up with what practitioners have known for centuries 🌊

🌑 new moon
energy is at its lowest. melatonin production is higher, sleep tends to be deeper. mood turns inward and reflective. hormones begin a fresh cycle — estrogen and testosterone are at their baseline, making this a natural reset for the body and mind.

🌒 waxing crescent & first quarter
energy slowly builds. sleep becomes lighter as the moon grows. mood lifts and motivation starts to rise. estrogen and testosterone begin climbing — you may notice more drive, creativity, and social energy.

🌔 waxing gibbous
the body is ramping up. sleep can feel restless as the full moon approaches. emotions intensify and the mind becomes more active. hormone levels are peaking and that buildup is very real — mentally and physically.

🌕 full moon
peak energy and peak intensity. studies show people sleep less and less deeply, melatonin dips, and the nervous system is more activated. emotions rise to the surface, mood swings are more common, and both estrogen and testosterone are at their highest — which can mean heightened intuition, passion, and sometimes tension.

🌖 waning gibbous
the body begins to exhale. sleep starts to stabilize. emotionally this phase brings clarity after the full moon intensity. hormones start their gradual decline — a natural invitation to slow down and reflect.

🌘 last quarter & waning crescent
energy is pulling back. sleep deepens again and the body craves rest. mood turns quieter and more introspective. hormone levels are winding down, making this the best time to release, cleanse, and restore.

your body already feels every phase of this cycle — witchcraft just gives you a framework to work with it intentionally 🖤

the moon has a cycle. so do you. honor both 🌙🌸
